# TMS-2290: ETAs and dock appointments off by one hour around daylight-saving changes

**Component:** fleetroute – ETA engine (`internal/eta`) · **Priority:** High · **Type:** Bug

## Context

Customer-facing ETAs and dock-appointment times are generated by
`eta.Planner.Estimate()`. Every spring and autumn the control tower gets a wave
of escalations: trucks show up an hour "late" or "early" against their ETA, and
dock appointments at hubs with a receiving window are booked at 05:00 or 07:00
instead of the 06:00 opening. Last week's examples (all times local to the hub):

* Hamburg → Berlin, departing 01:30 on 29 March 2026 with 2 h of driving: we
  quoted 03:30; the truck arrived at 04:30 (the clocks went forward at 02:00).
* Denver → Chicago, departing 23:30 MDT on 31 Oct 2026 with 3 h driving: we
  quoted 03:30 CST; the truck arrived at 02:30 CST and `Transit` said 4 h.
* Arrival at London Dagenham (window 06–22) at 23:00 BST on 24 Oct 2026: the
  dock appointment was booked for 05:00 GMT on the 25th. It should be 06:00 GMT.
* Arrival at Newark (window 06–22) at 23:10 EST on 7 March 2026: appointment
  booked for 07:00 EDT; should be 06:00 EDT.

## Expected behaviour

* Drive time, statutory breaks (`MaxContinuousDrive`/`Break`) and dwell are
  **real elapsed durations**. Two hours of driving always means the arrival
  instant is exactly two hours after the departure instant (plus breaks),
  whatever the wall clocks do in between.
* Receiving windows (`OpenHour`/`CloseHour`) are **local wall-clock hours** of the
  hub on the calendar day in question. If a truck arrives before opening, the
  trailer is accepted at that day's opening; at or after closing, at the next
  calendar day's opening (`OpenHour:00` local time, even if that night has a
  DST change). 24/7 hubs accept immediately.
* `Stop.Arrival`, `Stop.Accepted`, `Stop.Depart` and `Result.ETA` stay expressed
  in the hub's time zone; `Result.Transit` is the elapsed time from departure to ETA.
* Behaviour outside DST transitions must not change (existing tests).

## Constraints

* Do not change exported types, constants or function signatures in `internal/eta`.
* Standard library only; run `go test ./...`.
